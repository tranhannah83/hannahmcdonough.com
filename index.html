About Me

I'm a licensed cosmetology professional with 20 years of experience in the beauty industry, a professional Permanent Makeup Trainer, and a passionate nail artist and educator.

As a Permanent Makeup Trainer, I specialize in brows, lips, eyeliner, lash extensions, lash lifts, and brow lamination with color tint. I'm passionate about helping beauty professionals develop their skills, confidence, and creativity through hands-on education and professional training.

My experience also includes advanced nail artistry, nail structure, and professional nail education. Throughout my career, I've continued to grow, learn, and share my knowledge with others who are passionate about the beauty industry.

I believe that beauty is not only about creating beautiful results—it's also about education, confidence, precision, and helping others discover what they're capable of achieving. After 20 years in the industry, my passion for beauty and education continues to inspire everything I do.
